## What you will do
Look up a word you do not know. The glossary explains every hard word in a short sentence and lists the guides that use it.

## Before you start
You do not need any special role. Every guide links the first use of a hard word to this glossary, so you can also click the word inside a guide.

## Steps
1. Click **Help centre** in the side menu.
2. Click **Words explained** on the Help centre page.
3. Type part of the word in the **Filter terms** box.
4. Click **Filter** to narrow the list.
5. Read the short meaning under the word.
6. Click a guide id under the meaning to open the guide that uses the word.
7. Click a letter in **Jump to a letter** to skip ahead in the full list.

## What you should see
The list shows only the words that match what you typed, each with a one sentence meaning. Words are in A to Z order.

## If something goes wrong
- **No terms match.** Check the spelling or try a shorter part of the word. Plain words people use, such as thumbnail or bin, also find the right term. [Statuses and badges](help:R06)
- **A word on a badge is missing.** Badge words are explained one by one in the status guide. [Status words](help:R06)

## What you will do
Find a screen and open it with one click. Menu items come first in side menu order, then the screens you reach from inside another screen.

## Before you start
The side menu only shows the screens your role can open. If a link below says you cannot open it, your role is too low. [Roles and permissions](help:R02)

## What you should see
The screen opens. Screens with a name in the form of a folder, such as one video or one series, open from the list that comes before them.

## If something goes wrong
- **A link shows a Not allowed page.** Your role is too low for that screen. Ask a Super admin to change it. [Roles and permissions](help:R02)
- **A screen you expect is not in the side menu.** It may be a screen you reach from inside another screen. Look for it in the list above. [Glossary](help:R01)

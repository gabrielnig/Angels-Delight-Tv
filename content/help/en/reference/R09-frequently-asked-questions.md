## What you will do
Find a quick answer to a common question. Each answer points to the guide that has the full steps.

## Before you start
Nothing is needed. If your question is about a word, use the glossary. If it is about a problem, start from the Troubleshooting path. [Glossary](help:R01)

## Steps
1. **Why can viewers not see my video?** It may still be a draft. Only a video that says **Published** can be watched. [Viewers cannot play](help:T04)
2. **How long does a new video take?** A few minutes for a short one. **Encoding** shows the stage. [Stuck on Encoding](help:T01)
3. **Why is Publish not working?** Something on **Before you publish** is not done. Fix each open item. [A video will not publish](help:T02)
4. **Do I have to approve captions?** Yes. A video cannot go live until its captions are approved, unless an Admin publishes with a reason. [Captions](help:T03)
5. **Can I get a deleted video back?** Yes, for 30 days. Open **Trash** and click **Restore**. [Undo a mistake](help:T07)
6. **Which roles are there?** Editor, Admin and Super admin. Each can do everything the role below can do. [Roles](help:R02)
7. **What does a coloured badge mean?** Green is done, blue is working, amber needs a person, red did not finish. [Status words](help:R06)
8. **What file types can I upload?** MP4, MOV and MKV. The size limit is under the upload box. [An upload keeps failing](help:T05)
9. **I lost my phone. Can I still sign in?** Use a recovery code, or ask a Super admin to reset it. [Cannot sign in](help:T06)
10. **Where is a screen?** The screen map lists every screen with a link. [Screen map](help:R03)
11. **Which keys save time?** Press the question mark for Help, and the slash on a Help page to search. [Keyboard shortcuts](help:R05)
12. **Who do I ask when I am stuck?** Use **Message ARKDEV** on the Help centre home page and say what you tried. [What to send](help:T11)

## What you should see
You find your question, read the short answer and open the guide for the full steps.

## If something goes wrong
- **Your question is not here.** Search the Help centre, or use **Message ARKDEV** on the home page. [What to send](help:T11)
- **An answer does not match your screen.** Tell ARKDEV which question and what you see. [What is new](help:R08)

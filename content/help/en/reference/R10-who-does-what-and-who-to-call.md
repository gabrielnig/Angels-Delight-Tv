## What you will do
Learn which jobs are yours and which belong to ARKDEV, so you know who to call and do not wait for the wrong person.

## Before you start
Nothing is needed. Your contact for ARKDEV is on the Help centre home page, in the strip that says **Cannot find what you need?** It shows the email address or link set in **Site settings**. [Contact setting](help:R13)

## Steps
1. You look after the videos: upload, details, [captions](glossary:caption), [series](glossary:series), categories and Home. The guides in Daily and Weekly cover these.
2. You look after people: invite staff, set each [role](glossary:role) and remove people who leave. **Roles and invites** is the screen.
3. You look after the look and words of the site: **Theme**, **Site settings**, **Languages** and the legal pages.
4. You look after money screens: [sponsors](glossary:sponsor) and [donations](glossary:donation), including settling a gift that needs review.
5. You pay for and renew the accounts the site stands on: the [domain](glossary:domain), the [Cloudflare](glossary:cloudflare) plan and the server. ARKDEV can remind you.
6. You keep a second copy of your original video files on your own drive. The site keeps originals, but a second copy protects you.
7. ARKDEV looks after updates: putting new versions of the site live and checking they work.
8. ARKDEV looks after [backups](glossary:backup): they run every night, and ARKDEV restores from one if something is lost.
9. ARKDEV looks after the secret keys and passwords that connect the parts of the site, and changes them when needed.
10. ARKDEV looks after the server, the database and the tools that prepare videos and captions.
11. Call ARKDEV when **Retry** does not clear a red **Failed** badge, the site is down, or no video plays for anyone.
12. Do not call for a task in a guide. Search the Help centre first, then use **Message ARKDEV**.

## What you should see
You know for each job whether you do it or ARKDEV does. Anything about keys, updates, backups or the server goes to ARKDEV.

## If something goes wrong
- **ARKDEV does not answer.** Use the contact link again and say it is urgent and what is not working. Keep the reference text, the time and the screen name ready.
- **Your contact line is empty.** Ask the owner to add it. [Contact setting](help:R13)

## What you will do
Learn the keys that work in the admin area and in the video player. You can do everything in the admin area with the keyboard alone.

## Before you start
Keys that are one letter only work when your cursor is not inside a text box. The player list below is made from the same table the player help dialog uses.

## Steps
<!-- kb:generated R05 -->
1. Press **Tab** to move to the next button, link or field, and **Shift and Tab** to go back.
2. Press **Tab** once on a new page to reach **Skip to content**, then **Enter** to jump past the side menu.
3. Press **Enter** or **Space** to use the button or link you are on.
4. Press **Escape** to close a dialog, a menu or the Help panel.
5. Press **?** to open the Help panel for the screen you are on.
6. Press **/** on a Help centre page to jump to the search box.
7. Press **Left** or **Right**, or **Home** and **End**, to move between tabs in a row of tabs.
8. Player, press **Space** or **K**: Play or pause.
9. Player, press **Left** or **Right**: Back or forward 5 seconds.
10. Player, press **J** or **L**: Back or forward 10 seconds.
11. Player, press **Up** or **Down**: Volume up or down.
12. Player, press **M**: Mute or unmute.
13. Player, press **C**: Captions on or off.
14. Player, press **F**: Full screen.
15. Player, press **0 to 9**: Jump to 0 to 90 percent.
16. Player, press **Shift ,** or **Shift .**: Slower or faster.
17. Player, press **N**: Next video.
18. Player, press **S**: Skip intro, recap or credits.
19. Player, press **T**: Theatre mode.
20. Player, press **Esc**: Close menus or leave full screen.
21. Player, press **?**: Open this list.
<!-- /kb:generated -->

## What you should see
The key does what the list says. In the player, the keys work while the player has the focus, after you click it or tab to it.

## If something goes wrong
- **A letter key types in a box instead of acting.** Press **Escape**, click outside the box and try the key again. [Screen map](help:R03)
- **The player does not react to a key.** Click the video once so the player has the focus, then try again. [Glossary](help:R01)

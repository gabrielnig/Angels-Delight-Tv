## What you will do
Work through a short list that gets you from handover to confident owner. The list is in three parts: the first day, the first week and the first month.

## Before you start
You need a Super admin account with [two step sign in](glossary:two-step-sign-in) working. Keep a list of who owns each account: the domain, Cloudflare, the server and the email service. Read who to call in [Who does what](help:R10).

## Steps
1. First day: sign in and check your email codes arrive. If you use an authenticator app, save your [recovery codes](glossary:recovery-code) away from your phone.
2. First day: open the **Dashboard** and read what needs your attention.
3. First day: open **Site settings** and check the site name and your **Help centre contact**. [Contact setting](help:R13)
4. First day: open **Roles and invites** and check that you know every person with a staff role.
5. First day: open the viewer site on your phone and play one video all the way through.
6. First week: delete the test videos. Move each to **Trash**, then delete it for good, and check **System** shows less storage.
7. First week: upload and publish one real video to practise the whole path from upload to publish.
8. First week: ask ARKDEV to show you the last [backup](glossary:backup) and confirm a restore was tried at least once. [Routine care](help:R11)
9. First week: confirm the [uptime monitor](glossary:uptime-monitor) emails reach you, by asking ARKDEV to send a test alert.
10. First week: write down when the [domain](glossary:domain) renews and who pays for it.
11. First week: check the [Cloudflare](glossary:cloudflare) plan. Move to the paid plan before many viewers arrive.
12. First month: read **Analytics** and note which videos viewers finish.
13. First month: open **System** and read **Storage**, then decide when you need more space.
14. First month: tidy staff accounts. Remove people who no longer help, and check each role is the lowest one they need.
15. First month: ask ARKDEV about updates, and agree how you want to hear about them. [Who does what](help:R10)

## What you should see
At the end you know where your videos are, who can change what, how you would know if the site stopped, and who to call. Nothing here needs technical skill.

## If something goes wrong
- **You cannot find an item.** Use the screen map to find where each screen lives. [Screen map](help:R03)
- **You are not sure who should do a task.** The list of who does what answers it. [Who does what](help:R10)

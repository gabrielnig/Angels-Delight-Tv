## What you will do
Look up who may do a task. This list is made from the same permission table the admin area uses, so it cannot drift from what the site really allows.

## Before you start
There are three staff roles: [Editor](glossary:editor), [Admin](glossary:admin) and [Super admin](glossary:super-admin). Each role can do everything the roles below it can do. A person's [role](glossary:role) is set in **Roles and invites**.

## What you should see
You find the task and the lowest role that may do it. A task for a higher role shows a grey button with the reason, or the menu item is hidden.

## If something goes wrong
- **A button is grey and says your role cannot do it.** Ask a Super admin to change your role in **Roles and invites**. [Roles](help:R03)
- **A menu item is missing.** The screen needs a higher role than yours. The screen map lists each screen. [Screen map](help:R03)

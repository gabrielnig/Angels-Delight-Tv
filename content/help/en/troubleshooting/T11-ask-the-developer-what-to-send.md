## What you will do
Write a short message that tells ARKDEV what went wrong, so they can fix it without asking you ten questions. You end with a message ready to send.

## Before you start
Try the matching troubleshooting guide first. Many problems end with a **Retry** button. Open the Help centre home page and find the strip **Cannot find what you need?** It holds the contact for ARKDEV.

## Steps
1. Say who you are and which role you have, for example Editor or Admin.
2. Write the reference text. This is the exact words of the red message you saw, copied or typed as you read them.
3. For a viewer problem, add the [reference code](glossary:reference-code) the viewer saw under the message. It looks like AD-7K4Q.
4. Write the time and date it happened, with the time zone. Say Lagos time if you are in Nigeria.
5. Name the screen, for example **Encoding** or **Captions**, and the video or person it was about.
6. Say what you were doing just before, in one or two short sentences.
7. Say how many people are affected: only you, a few viewers or everyone.
8. Add a picture of the screen if you can, with no passwords, codes or personal details in it.
9. Say what you already tried, such as **Retry** or signing out and in.
10. Send the message with the **Message ARKDEV** button on the Help centre home page, or to the contact your owner gave you.

## What you should see
Your message is short, has the exact error words, the time, the screen and what you tried. ARKDEV can usually answer with the fix or with one question.

> [!CAREFUL]
> Never send a password, a 6 digit code from your app, a recovery code or a full card number. ARKDEV will never ask for them.

## If something goes wrong
- **There is no Message ARKDEV button.** The contact is not set yet. Ask the owner to add it in **Site settings**.
- **You cannot copy the red message.** Take a picture of the screen instead, and type the words too if you can.

## Account
Your own sign in on the platform. It has an email address, a password and, for staff, a two step sign in.
Aliases: login, profile

## Admin
A staff role above Editor. Admins also look after settings, the theme, users and money screens. The word also names the staff area of the site.
Aliases: administrator, role

## Analytics
Numbers about how people watch your videos, such as views and watch time.
Aliases: statistics, stats, numbers

## Approved
Captions that a person checked, so viewers can see them.
Guides: D07

## Authenticator app
An app on your phone that shows a new 6 digit code every 30 seconds. You type that code when you sign in.
Aliases: two step app, code app

## Backup
A safe copy of the site data, made every night and kept in a separate place. ARKDEV can restore from it if something is lost.
Guides: R11
Aliases: restore, copy

## Caption
The words shown on screen while a video plays. Some people call them subtitles.
Guides: D07
Aliases: subtitle, subtitles, cc

## Category
A group of videos on the site, such as Farming. People use categories to browse.
Aliases: genre, topic

## Cloudflare
The company that delivers your videos to viewers quickly and keeps the site address working. You log in to it to see the plan and the domain.
Guides: T17
Aliases: cdn, media host

## Creative
One sponsor message with its headline, text, link, pictures and the places it shows.
Aliases: sponsor message, advert
Guides: M03

## Cue
One line of a caption with its start and end time.
Aliases: caption line, subtitle line

## Dashboard
The first screen after you sign in. It shows what needs your attention.
Guides: S04

## Domain
The web address of the site, such as the name people type in a browser. It is rented for a year at a time and must be renewed.
Guides: R11
Aliases: web address, url, website name

## Donation
A gift of money from a viewer, made through the payment service. You can see each gift on the Donations screen.
Guides: T09
Aliases: gift, giving, payment
Guides: M01, M08

## Draft
A video that is not visible to viewers yet.
Aliases: unpublished

## Editor
A staff role. Editors look after videos, captions and Home.
Aliases: role

## Encoding
The step after an upload when the platform prepares a video for playing. It is also called processing.
Aliases: processing, converting

## Episode
One video inside a series. Some series call it a lesson or a chapter.
Aliases: lesson, chapter

## Exports
Files you download from the admin area, such as a list of views or donations.
Aliases: download, report

## Failed
A word on a badge that means the work did not finish. Most failed items have a Retry button.
Guides: T01
Aliases: error, did not finish

## Group
One section of a series, such as Part 1 or Book 2. The owner chooses the word. Videos sit inside groups.
Guides: W20, W24

## Hero
The large featured picture or clip at the top of the viewer Home page.
Aliases: banner, featured

## Home curation
The admin screen where you choose which rows viewers see on Home and in what order.
Aliases: home page, rows

## Impression
One time a sponsor card was half visible for one second. Sponsor reports count them.
Aliases: view, sponsor view
Guides: M06

## Invite
An email that lets a new staff member set up an account with the role you chose.
Aliases: add staff

## Item
One video inside a group of a series, such as Lesson 3 or Chapter 3. The owner chooses the word.
Guides: W20

## Job
A task the site does in the background, such as getting a video ready, making captions or sending an email.
Guides: T15
Aliases: queue, background task

## Kids safe
A video rating that allows it to appear for kids. Kids profiles only show kids safe videos.
Aliases: kids, children, child safe, maturity

## Master switch
A single switch that turns a whole feature on or off for everyone. Only a Super admin can use it.
Aliases: feature switch, flag
Guides: M02

## Maturity rating
The age level you give a video, from the lowest rating to the highest. It decides who can watch it.
Aliases: rating, age rating, kids rating

## Name set
The words a series uses for its groups and items, such as Part and Lesson or Season and Episode.
Aliases: series names, labels
Guides: W21, W22

## Needs review
A caption state that means the captions are written but nobody has approved them yet.
Guides: T03
Aliases: review
Guides: M09

## Paystack
The payment company that takes gifts on its own secure page. Its keys are kept on the server, never in the admin area.
Aliases: payment service, payment company
Guides: M07, M10

## Placement
A place on the viewer pages where a sponsor message can show.
Aliases: slot, ad space
Guides: M04

## Poster
The still picture shown for a video before it plays.
Aliases: thumbnail, cover, picture

## Preview clip
A short silent clip that can play on the Home hero for viewers on fast connections.
Guides: T12
Aliases: trailer, hero clip

## Publish
Make a video visible to viewers.
Aliases: go live, release

## Published
A video that viewers can watch now. A video that is not published is a draft, scheduled or unpublished.
Aliases: live, go live

## Reach
The number of different viewers on one day. It is a daily figure and is never added up across days.
Guides: M06

## Recovery code
A code you saved when you set up two step sign in. Each one works once if you lose your phone.
Guides: T06
Aliases: backup code, lost phone code

## Reference code
A short code such as AD-7K4Q shown to a viewer when a video cannot play. Send it to ARKDEV so they can find the problem.
Guides: T11
Aliases: error code, support code

## Retry
A button that tries failed work again. It does not change your original file.
Aliases: try again

## Role
What a staff person is allowed to do. The roles are Editor, Admin and Super admin.
Guides: R02
Aliases: permission, access

## Scheduled
A video that will go live by itself at the date and time you chose.
Aliases: timed, later

## Season
A group of episodes inside a series. A series can have one season or many.
Aliases: part, module

## Section
One row on the viewer Home page, such as New this week. You can show, hide and reorder sections.
Aliases: row, shelf

## Series
A set of videos shown in order, such as a course with episodes.
Aliases: show, season, programme

## Session
The time you stay signed in on a device. It ends after a long quiet time, and Sign out everywhere ends all of them.
Aliases: signed in, logged in

## Side menu
The list of screens on the left of the admin. On a phone it opens from the menu button.
Guides: S04

## Sponsor
A business or person that pays to show a message beside your videos.
Aliases: advert, ad, advertiser
Guides: M01, M03

## Storage budget
The amount of file space you plan to use. The System screen warns you when you are near it.
Guides: T10
Aliases: space, disk, storage limit

## Super admin
The highest staff role. Super admins also manage roles, switches, failed jobs and the Audit log security part.
Aliases: owner, super administrator

## Test mode
A way of running donations with a test key, so no real money moves. Test gifts show a Test badge and are not counted.
Aliases: sandbox, test gift
Guides: M10, M11

## Theme
The colours, fonts and look of the viewer site.
Aliases: look, colours, brand

## Transcript
The captions of a video written out as plain text, so you can read them without playing it.
Aliases: script, text

## Trash
Where deleted videos wait for 30 days before they are gone for good.
Aliases: bin, deleted

## Trending
A viewer row of videos that many people are watching right now. It refreshes every hour.
Aliases: popular, top ten

## Two step sign in
A second proof when you sign in: a 6 digit code from your phone after your password. All staff accounts need it.
Guides: T06
Aliases: 2fa, two factor, verification code

## Unpublish
Take a published video off the viewer site without deleting it.
Aliases: hide, take down

## Upload
Add a video file from your computer to the platform.
Guides: D02
Aliases: import, add video

## Uptime monitor
A free service that checks the site every minute and sends an email if it stops answering.
Guides: R11
Aliases: monitor, site down alert

## Viewer
Anyone who watches videos on the site, with or without an account.
Aliases: learner, audience, user

## Workers plan
The Cloudflare plan that delivers your videos. The free plan stops at a daily limit and the paid plan raises it.
Guides: T17
Aliases: cloudflare plan, plan limit

## Audit log
A list of who did what in the admin and when. Nobody can edit or delete an entry.
Guides: K06

## Guest
A visitor who has not signed in. Guests can watch if the site allows it.
Aliases: visitor, no account, anonymous
Guides: K03

## Kids mode
A way of watching that shows only videos rated G. Kids profiles use it, and visitors can turn it on for their device.
Aliases: kids, children, kids profile, child safe
Guides: K01

## Suspend
Stop an account from signing in without deleting anything. A suspension can be undone.
Aliases: ban, block, lock
Guides: K04

## Receipt
The email sent to a donor after a successful gift, with the amount, date and reference.
Aliases: thank you email
Guides: M13

## Transparency page
A public page that shows donors how gifts are used.
Aliases: how gifts are used
Guides: M17

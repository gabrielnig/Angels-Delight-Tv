## What you will do
Find a guide with the search box, open it, mark it as done and say whether it helped.

## Before you start
- **Who can do this:** every staff member.
- **Time:** about 2 minutes.
- The **Help centre** link is at the bottom of the side menu. Each screen also has a **Help** link at the top that opens the guide for that screen.
- Every guide with a video shows the video first, then the steps and pictures. The words of the video are written under it.
- To keep a copy, choose **Print or save as PDF** on a guide, or **Download PDF** when it is offered. An offline copy of all guides is made by your developer at each release. Ask for it if you need to read without a connection.

## Steps
<!-- kb:steps S08 -->
1. Choose **Help centre** in the side menu.
<!-- /kb:steps -->

## What you should see
The guide shows **Done. Mark as not done**, and the question **Was this helpful?** shows your answer. Your progress on the Help centre home counts the guides you finished.

> [!TIP]
> Press the slash key on your keyboard to jump to the search box from anywhere in the help centre.

## If something goes wrong
- **Nothing is found.** Try a shorter or plainer word, such as "poster" or "captions". You can also open a learning path from the Help centre home.
- **A step does not match your screen.** Your site may be newer than the guide. Tell your developer which guide it is.
- **Print or save as PDF does nothing.** Your browser may block the print box. Allow it for this site and try again.

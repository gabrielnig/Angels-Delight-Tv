Sign in and set up two step sign in (S02)
For everyone. 1 min 2 s.

0:03  Go to the sign in page, "/admin/login".
0:09  Type your email in the Email box.
0:15  Type your password in the Password box.
0:22  Click Sign in.
      The pointer moves to Sign in.
0:27  Wait until you see Enter your code.
0:34  Type the 6 digit code from your app in the Digit 1 of 6 box.
      The pointer moves to Digit 1 of 6.
0:41  Click Verify.
      The pointer moves to Verify.
0:46  Wait until you see Dashboard. You are signed in.

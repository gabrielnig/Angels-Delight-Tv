# Netflix audit and gap analysis (24 November 2026)

Method: the REA approach (github.com/morluto/rea, Reverse Engineer Anything) applied to public sources only: Netflix Help Center, Netflix Tech Blog, the logged-out netflix.com page and its shipped CSS, and trade writing. No app was decompiled and no DRM or login was touched. Each claim carries a confidence tag: verified, likely, inferred. Signed-in browse pixel values are not public, so every number marked likely or inferred is a starting value to tune on real low end Android phones in Lagos.

## 1. Netflix feature inventory (verified unless marked)

- Guests: the logged-out page is a sign-up pitch, a trending strip and an FAQ. Guests cannot browse or play (inferred). Our platform deliberately differs: anyone browses and plays.
- Home: billboard with Play and More Info, maturity badge, mute toggle (likely). Autoplay previews is a per-profile setting. Rows: Continue Watching (removable), Top 10, New, genre rows, My List. Page is a 2D grid, vertical scanning dominates, row length adapts to device. TV focus shows runtime, rating, synopsis and callout labels.
- Personalisation: candidate rows, filter, rank, dedupe. Not needed at launch.
- Search and browse: genre filter inside search on TV, suggestions and empty states unverified.
- Title page: three level rating (not for me, like, love), Most Liked label, My List (2,000 cap, filter and sort on mobile), episodes, More Like This (likely).
- Player: Space or Enter play, F fullscreen, Left and Right 10 s, Up and Down volume, M mute, S skip intro, speed control, subtitle appearance (font, size, shadow, background), autoplay next episode toggle, Skip Recap (likely).
- Profiles: Kids profile (logo on avatar, no account access, no email, kids only titles, password to leave), per-profile maturity limit, block titles, profile PIN.
- Accessibility: audio description, customisable captions, screen readers, keyboard shortcuts, voice.
- Delivery: per-title encoding ladders, shot based keyframes, preview clips, AVA artwork frame choice.

## 2. Design system facts from public CSS (Hawkins consumer tokens)

- Type: variable font with weights 100 to 900, `font-display: swap`, rem sizes 0.8125, 1, 1.125, 1.25, 1.5, 2, 2.5, 3.5, 4, line height 1.25 on display and 1.5 on body. Netflix Sans is proprietary and must not be copied. Use an open face.
- Buttons: heights 2rem, 2.5rem, 3rem, radius 0.25rem, horizontal padding 1rem, border 1px, icon gap 0.5rem, icon sizes 1rem and 1.5rem. Disabled text 0.4 alpha, pressed 0.7 alpha.
- Motion: hover transition 250 ms with a strong ease-out, pressed 0 ms, reduced motion removes transitions. Hover only rules wrapped in `@media (hover: hover)`. Animate only transform and opacity.
- Breakpoints 600, 960, 1280, 1600, 1920, 2560. Spacing on a 4 px rhythm. Scrim alpha 0.85 to 0.9.
- Likely or inferred: landscape tile 16:9, poster 2:3, gutters about 4 percent of width, row gap about 3vw, card gap 4 to 8 px, columns about 6, 5, 4, 3, 2.5 by width, hover scale 1.3 to 1.5 with 300 to 500 ms delay and 200 to 300 ms duration, nav about 68 px transparent then solid after a few px of scroll, controls fade after 3 to 5 s, scrubber about 5 px, modal about 850 px, billboard 56 to 80vh with left and bottom gradients, "Explore all" link revealed on row hover, row arrows revealed on row hover, native scroll behaviour with a peeking next tile.

## 3. Recommendations for Angels Delight TV (adopt, adapt, skip)

Adopt: dark first token surface with configurable colours; 4 px spacing scale; button sizes above with 44 px minimum touch targets; 250 ms hover with strong ease-out and 0 ms pressed; reduced motion with the standard `reduce` value and an always visible focus ring; hover effects only under `hover: hover and pointer: fine`; breakpoints 600, 960, 1280, 1920 mobile first; card ratios 16:9 and 2:3 with explicit dimensions; hero scrim 0.85; animate only transform and opacity; container queries on rows.

Adapt: open variable font stack with Latin subset; type scale 0.8125 to 2.5 rem with hero `clamp(2rem, 5vw, 3.5rem)`; gutter `clamp(1rem, 4vw, 3.5rem)`; fractional columns so the next card peeks (2.3, 3.3, 4.3, 5.3, 6.3); native scroll snap rows with desktop arrow buttons; light hover card (title, length, subject, Play) after 300 ms with 1.25 scale, origin left for first card and right for last; nav 4 rem desktop and 3 rem mobile, solid after about 40 px scroll by IntersectionObserver; real detail pages with their own URL instead of modals; player controls fade after 3 s, scrubber 5 px growing to 8 px with 44 px hit area, Next lesson countdown 5 to 10 s cancellable; bottom tab bar on phones with safe area inset; skeletons at card ratio; empty and error states with one sentence and one action.

Skip: autoplay billboard video on every connection (allow an optional muted preview only when `saveData` is off and the connection is fast), row hover videos, Netflix brand treatments, personalisation rows, scrubber sprites beyond current, 2560 breakpoint, TV 10 foot layout at launch, removing the focus ring (the Netflix sign-in field does).

## 5. Guest access analysis (owner rule: no sign-up wall)

There is no auth middleware. Gates are in pages, layouts and handlers: `requireSession` and `requireProfile` (apps/web/src/lib/session.ts), `guard()` (apps/web/src/server/viewer/api.ts), `createRequestAuth.getViewer` (apps/web/src/server/auth/request-auth.ts), `PlaybackService.startSession` (packages/video/src/playback/service.ts), and `withAccount` plus `loadOwnedProfile` inside every `@ad/viewer` service. Signed media URLs (`signedMediaUrl`, MEDIA_URL_SECRET), the key endpoint (token only), the edge Worker (exp and sig only) and analytics already work without an account, so only entry points block guests: `/` shows the sign-in card, `/browse`, `/search`, `/watch/[videoId]` redirect, `/title` and `/series` show only `PublicTitle` for guests, `/api/home`, `/api/browse`, `/api/titles`, `/api/search`, `/api/play/[id]/session` return 401, and the playback token requires `aid`.

Plan: a `Viewer` union of account or guest. Signed `ad_guest` cookie (128 bit, HttpOnly, SameSite Lax, Secure, 180 days) set on first play, not every request. Guest playback token with `pid` null and a `gst` claim, never `pv`. Catalog services get an account-free path with an audience argument. Replace `requireProfile` with a viewer-or-guest helper on Home, Browse, Search, Title, Series, Watch. `publicGuard()` for public catalog GETs. Personal routes (progress, my-list, profiles, recent searches) stay 401. Guest progress in localStorage key `ad:guest:progress:v1`, cap 100, LRU, transport mode `local` in WatchClient, optional one time `POST /api/progress/import` after sign in (max 100 entries, validated, only fills missing or older). "Sign in to save" dialog for My List and likes with `next` preserved, no hard redirects. Signed-out header shows Sign in and Create account. `/my-list` for guests shows an explainer, not a redirect. Guests see the general audience by default, with a Kids mode toggle (a convenience, not a lock) and a site setting `guestAudience` (general or kids, default general). Abuse protection: per IP and per guest session limits, guest key limits, guest segment URL TTL 15 to 20 minutes (accounts keep 2 hours), Turnstile escalation after repeated limit hits, a Cloudflare rate rule documented in DEPLOYMENT.md. Analytics guest identity is a daily hash of the guest id. NDPA review brief must include the guest cookie and anonymous analytics id. SEO: public pages become fully indexable with real content, add robots, sitemap and VideoObject JSON-LD. No schema change, reversible. Cloudflare Workers free plan (about 1,200 plays a day) will be reached sooner.

Tests first: guest session service, token round trip with `gst`, request-auth guest principal while `getActor` still throws, public GETs 200 and personal GETs 401, guest cookie forgery, `getViewerContext` without redirect, `getWatchData` guest branch, localStorage progress, progress import, edge Worker still rejecting forged signatures, route authorization matrix.

Docs to update: SECURITY.md capability table and route tests, ARCHITECTURE.md playback token and TTL sections, USER-STORIES (M01-S13, M01-S14, M07-S06, M06 signed out search, M08-S01, S15, S16, M03-S01, S11, M05-S05, M19), DEPLOYMENT.md, CLIENT-INPUTS.md, ai-context.md.

Mobile header options on Home

Recommendation: Option B

  1. It fixes the clutter at the root. Four controls become three, and the loudest one (Create account) leaves the bar. The hero and the Play button become the first thing a visitor sees.
  2. One header for everyone. The guest bar and the signed in bar share a skeleton: logo, Search, then one account slot (guest icon or avatar), with the bell sitting before it for adults. People who sign in see no layout jump, and the code is one component with one slot.
  3. It survives other languages. Only icons sit in the bar. Yoruba, Igbo and Hausa labels live in the sheet rows and tab labels, where they have a full line to use (see the stress test).
  4. Sign in is not the goal of the Home page, watching is. Guests can watch. Sign up is offered where it matters: the save to My List dialog, the My List explainer, and the account sheet one tap away.
  5. Smallest build that keeps the design system. The tab bar, the 4 tab layout, the scroll to solid behaviour (design system 11.8) and the account bottom sheet (11.9) stay as written. The sheet is already specified for signed in users on phones, but the build still opens a popover there.

Keep from the other options: A's idea of a visible Sign in on the sheet as the first and only filled button, and C's transparent over hero look, which the design system already specifies for every option (clear at the top, solid after 40 px).

Findings. The four dotted letters are missing in all six families today, so Nigerian titles fall back to a system font. The vietnamese subset fixes them. Precomposed ṣ and the naira sign stay missing in DM Sans, Lora and Poppins after S12, and the S12 coverage test expects ṣ in every family. Two ways out: store or normalise text as NFD so ṣ renders as s plus U+0323, or accept a system fallback for those two characters and relax the test.

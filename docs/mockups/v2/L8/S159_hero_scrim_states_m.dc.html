State 5 · Dots and the Pause control: rotating, paused, reduced motion and Data saver

Controls and variants

Rotating
Pause slideshow
Pause shows the pause icon. Current dot is accent and 12 px.
Paused
Play slideshow
Hover, focus or touch pauses with no change to the control. The control button toggles it for good.
Reduced motion
No rotation, so no Pause control. Dots and arrows move slides. Swap, no fade.
Data saver
No rotation, no clip, no prefetch of the next slide, no Pause control. Dots, arrows and swipe still work and the next image loads when asked.
Dots have a 44 px hit area. Current dot carries aria-current. Pause is a button with the visible words and an icon, label "Pause slideshow".

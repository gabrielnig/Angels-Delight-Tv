Cookies and device storage

You can watch without an account. When you do, we keep very little, and none of it names you.

Cookies

__Host-ad_guest
Limits misuse and lets video play for people without an account.
Lasts up to 180 days
__Host-ad_gaud
Remembers whether Kids mode is on for this device.
Lasts up to 180 days

Anonymous daily id

To count views, we make a code from the guest cookie and today's date. The code changes every day, so it cannot follow you from one day to the next. We do not keep the cookie value. While Kids mode is on, we store no code at all.

Saved on this device

Your places and Continue learning stay in this browser. They leave your device only if you choose to add them to an account. You can remove them at any time from the menu.

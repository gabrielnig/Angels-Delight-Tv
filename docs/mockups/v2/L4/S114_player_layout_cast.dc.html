Should, behind spike

AirPlay behaviour (Safari)

  • Behind a device spike (R16). The button is built behind a setting that stays off until the spike passes on a real iPhone and Apple TV. Until then no AirPlay button shows anywhere.
  • Starts from the AirPlay button. The player reloads the video through the native stream at the same time, then opens the system picker.
  • Position and play state are kept through the switch.
  • Quality is chosen by the TV, so the Quality row in Settings is hidden while casting and comes back when the viewer presses Play on this screen.
  • Captions follow the TV. The Captions button stays.
  • Back to this screen when the viewer stops AirPlay in the picker. Press Play to continue here.
  • No sign in needed. No third party script, no account, no new permissions.

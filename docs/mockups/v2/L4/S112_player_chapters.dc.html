What the viewer sees

  • Rail rests at 5 px with a 2 px gap at each chapter start. Buffered and played fills follow the segments.
  • Hit area is 44 px tall (see S113 for the zone drawn).
  • Current chapter shows beside the time on desktop: "2:14 / 5:52 Warm air rises". Long titles stop at 24 characters with an ellipsis.
  • Chapters button (list icon) sits between Captions and Settings. It is hidden when a video has no chapters.
  • Chapters are optional. A video with none looks exactly like today.

A

Admin
A person who signs in to this area. Admin is also a role. Admins can change settings and approve captions for many videos at once.
See guide: Roles and who can do what
Audit log
A list of who did what in the admin, and when. Only Super admins can read it.
See guide: Read the audit log

C

Captions
The words shown on screen as a person speaks. Viewers can turn them on or off.
See guide: Approve captions for a new video
Category
A subject group such as Health or Money. A video can be in more than one.
See guide: Tidy categories and series

D

Data saver
A viewer setting that keeps video at 480p or lower so it uses less data.
See guide: Check how many people watched
Draft
A video you have not published. Viewers cannot see it.
See guide: Publish a video

E

Encoding
The wait after an upload while the site gets the video ready to play. Short videos take a few minutes.
See guide: A video is stuck in Encoding
Editor
A role that can manage videos, captions and Home rows. Editors cannot change site settings.
See guide: Roles and who can do what

H

Home row
A line of videos on the Home page, such as Continue learning.
See guide: Update the Home rows

K

Kids mode
A setting that shows only videos marked for all ages and hides sponsors and gifts.
See guide: What Kids mode hides

M

Maturity label
An age label on a video, such as G, PG, 12, 15 or 18. Guests see up to 12 by default.
See guide: Set an age label on a video

P

Publish
Make a video visible to viewers. You can unpublish it again.
See guide: Publish a video

S

Series
A group of videos that belong together, in order.
See guide: Tidy categories and series
Sponsor
A business that pays to show a short card to viewers. Sponsors are off by default.
See guide: Turn sponsors on

T

Trash
Where deleted videos wait for 30 days before they are gone for good. You can bring them back.
See guide: Remove a video and bring it back
Two step sign in
A second check after your password, with a code from your phone.
See guide: Turn on two step sign in
